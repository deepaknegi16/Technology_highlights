How to use these guides

  • Every topic card has Why interviewers ask, a plain-English explanation, an animated diagram you can step through, pitfalls and likely follow-ups.
  • Press ? inside a guide for keyboard shortcuts (/ search, t tough-only, j/k next/prev card, ←/→ step an animation).
  • Tick Read on cards and Solved on problems; quizzes save answers. Use the ⚙ menu in a guide to export/import progress as JSON.
